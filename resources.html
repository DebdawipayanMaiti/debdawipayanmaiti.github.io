Biophysics Resources

A working collection of course material, papers, active research groups, tools, and opportunities for anyone studying experimental — and a bit of theoretical — biophysics. I put this together while building my own background in the field, and I keep adding to it as I find things worth sharing.

Note: a few entries below are pre-signed publisher PDF links or Google search results saved from my own browsing — those can expire or shift over time. Where that happens, search the title directly on the publisher's site or Google Scholar.
